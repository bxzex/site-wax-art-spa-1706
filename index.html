Frequently Asked Questions

We highly recommend booking an appointment to guarantee your spot, as we work mostly off of appointments to ensure personalized care. We do accept walk-ins when availability permits, but you may be asked to schedule for a later time.

We use premium, hypoallergenic hard and soft waxes depending on the area being treated and your skin's sensitivity. Our products are designed to minimize discomfort and leave your skin perfectly smooth.

Please ensure your hair is at least 1/4 inch long (about the size of a grain of rice). Gently exfoliate the area 24 hours before your appointment, and avoid applying lotions or oils on the day of your service.

We are conveniently located in Oasis Plaza at 2862 NE 8th St, Homestead, FL 33033. There is ample parking available for our guests.
